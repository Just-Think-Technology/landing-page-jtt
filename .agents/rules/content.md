# Content Rules

JTT is a technology company focused on software and digital systems that solve real business problems.

Voice: direct, intelligent, confident, precise, modern and business-aware.

Avoid agency clichés, empty buzzwords and unsupported superlatives.

Never invent:
- metrics
- customer outcomes
- awards
- certifications
- partnerships
- testimonials
- technologies used by clients

Use approved company material as the source for factual claims.
